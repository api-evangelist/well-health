# WELL Health Technologies

> WELL Health Technologies Corp (TSX: WELL, OTCQX: WHTCF) is a Vancouver, Canada-based healthcare technology company — Canada's largest outpatient medical clinic owner-operator and a leading digital health service provider. Its programmatic surface is the apps.health marketplace, through which third-party digital health apps integrate with WELL's network of EMRs (OSCAR Pro, Profile) using HL7 FHIR and SMART on FHIR. There is no self-serve public developer portal, sandbox, OpenAPI, or FHIR CapabilityStatement; integration is gated behind a partner/contact and conformance-testing process.

## Integration
- [apps.health for Developers](https://apps.health/for-developers): partner intake form and overview of the EMR integration program.
- [How to get your product on apps.health](https://apps.health/how-to-get-your-product-on-apps-health/): the full integration process — discovery, developer documentation, conformance testing, privacy & security review, partner agreement, marketplace profile.
- Integration standard: OSCAR Pro exposes an open FHIR-based API and SMART on FHIR HL7 infrastructure. FHIR reference: https://www.hl7.org/fhir/overview.html

## Notes for agents
- No public OpenAPI, GraphQL, MCP server, or FHIR metadata endpoint is available (all probed paths return 404). API access requires becoming a vetted apps.health partner.
- Supported EMRs: OSCAR Pro, Profile. Reach: 2,000+ clinics and 10,000+ healthcare professionals across Canada.
